## Scope
Home page (`apps/web/src/app/(main)/page.tsx`) — Persuade. The world it sets carries to search, property detail, auth and dashboard (Operate).

## Audience and job
Seekers (buy/rent/PG/commercial) start a locality search; listers start posting. Primary action: the working locality search with listing-type tabs. Secondary: post a property.

## Constraints
Homepage stats and city counts are placeholders kept by the user. No fabricated testimonials. Hero aerial footage `public/assets/hero-bg.mp4` is kept as an asset. Remove emoji and decorative icon tiles.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Property search set as an Indian stamp-paper document: listings are deeds, verification is a violet registry seal. Refuses the full-bleed video hero with a glass search box and icon-tile feature grid.
OWN-WORLD: Pale stamp-paper green ground, green guilloche rosette borders, deed-black ink, registry violet for seals and the primary action, nothing else. Rozha One display caps like a stamp-paper header, Hind for all UI, typewriter mono only for registration numbers. Double hairline rules, ruled entry lines, round violet seals at a slight angle.
STORY: The visitor sees a document already filled in with their search, understands every listing carries its papers, types a locality, and lands on results set as deed slips.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One wide stamp-paper sheet inside a guilloche frame filling the viewport under a thin nav. Sheet header: denomination-style heading on the left, the aerial footage pasted as an affixed photograph on the right with a violet seal overlapping its corner. Listing-type tabs as typed clauses, then the locality field on a ruled line with the violet Search button at its end. Placeholder figures typed as a schedule along the sheet foot.
FORM: Stamp Paper & Registry, position 1 of 7 on my ordered list (chosen by the user over the roll). seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
